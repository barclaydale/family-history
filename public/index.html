Milwaukee & Waukesha Counties, from 1842

The Mueller Family

Carl and Elizabeth Mueller landed in New York on July 26, 1842 with six children, and bought eighty acres in Milwaukee County a month later. What follows is five generations of what happened next — a Civil War captain buried in Georgia, a midwife who delivered two thousand babies, a social club with a place on Pewaukee Lake, a lithography company, and a great many painters.
